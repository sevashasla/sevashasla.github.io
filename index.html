Vsevolod Skorokhodov

I am an M.Sc. student in Data Science at EPFL, with a strong interest in Computer Vision, 3D, Generative Modeling, and Efficient Inference.

Recently, I interned at the Schindler EPFL Lab, where I worked on multimodal foundation geometry models and on developing methods to generate simulation-ready 3D models directly from RGB images. Prior to this, I was a research student at CVLab under the supervision of Prof. Pascal Fua, where I focused on Gaussian splatting and diffusion models. Before joining EPFL, I earned my B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and Physics at MIPT. During my undergraduate studies, my research centered on Uncertainty Estimation in Semantic Neural Radiance Fields.

I open to new connections and collaborations. Feel free to reach out via email or connect with me on GitHub or Telegram.
